How it works

Three steps. You only ever do one at a time.

recallit holds the schedule so you don't have to keep it in your head.

1

Describe it

Drop in PDFs and links, or name a topic. It builds the cards and cites the line each one came from. What it can't verify, it holds back and shows you.

2

Practice it

Drill, or talk it through. Either way you get a straight grade: the points you got, the ones you missed, and the line it checked.

3

Remember it

The right card is waiting when you come back. Nothing to schedule, nothing to gamify.
